# Check Whether an MME or AMF Is Overloaded

1. In **Operators**, open the operator.
2. In **Core Network Nodes**, click the name of the MME or AMF, or click its view icon in **Actions**.
3. Read **Overload**. **None** means the node is not overloaded. Otherwise it shows the traffic the node asked to reject, for each GUMMEI of an MME, or for each group of GUAMIs of an AMF and each of its slices.
