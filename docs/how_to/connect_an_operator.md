# Connect an Operator

1. In **Operators**, click **Create**, then enter the operator's name and PLMNs.
2. Open the operator. Next to **Gateway identity**, click the edit icon, turn on **4G** and enter the Global eNB ID the operator assigned to you.
3. In **Core network nodes**, click **Add**, choose **MME** and enter the MME's addresses.
4. Ask the operator to accept that Global eNB ID and the TAIs of your radios on its MMEs.
5. [Connect a radio](connect_a_radio.md). The MME's status turns **Up**.
