Interim user-plane results, Ella MOCN on GCP (2026-11-15, run 1 of 3, S1, 1024 sessions, 64 eNBs, 16 S-GWs)
Hosts: 3 x n2-standard-8, gVNIC (2 RX/2 TX queues per vNIC), Ubuntu 26.04, kernel 7.0.0-1017-gcp, MTU 1500.
Ella MOCN host: NIC interrupts spread over the 8 vCPUs, nohz=off (CPU accounting), nftables forward drop.
RFC 2544 binary search at 0% loss, 10 s trials, 1% resolution, 30 s confirmation. Single run, not yet a median.

Aggregated (through Ella MOCN), all on the eBPF fast path (slow-path and dropped counters 0):

Direct baseline (generator to receiver without Ella MOCN), exploratory: 4.0 Mpps lossless at 46 B uplink.
Latency, exploratory (200 probes, 84 B): direct 0.340 ms avg RTT, aggregated 0.505 ms avg RTT (+0.165 ms).

Bottleneck so far: each vNIC of n2-standard-8 with 2 vNICs gets only 2 RX queues, so 2 vCPUs carry each
direction; the knee is those 2 vCPUs saturating (~0.5-0.65 Mpps each). RPS does not help (TX queue contention).

(rx_no_buffers_posted), with no vCPU saturated: the 0%-loss rate is bounded by burst absorption, not by CPU.
